Who We Are

PestPro is an independent pest control referral network and informational website. We are not a licensed pest control operator. We do not employ pest control technicians, perform treatments, or dispatch service vehicles. Instead, we connect homeowners, renters, and property managers across the United States with pre-screened, state-licensed pest control professionals in their local area.

Our platform helps you quickly identify the pest issue you are facing, understand your treatment options, and get in touch with a qualified local expert who can assess your property and provide a professional solution.

How It Works

  1. You submit a service request through our website or call our toll-free number.
  2. We match your request to one or more licensed pest control companies in your area.

Licensing & Regulatory Compliance

Pest control services in the United States are regulated at the state level. Each state requires pest control operators to obtain a license from its Department of Agriculture or equivalent regulatory body. All pesticides used must be registered with the U.S. Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) under the Federal Insecticide, Fungicide, and Rodenticide Act (FIFRA). Applicators are required to follow label instructions, which are legally binding under federal law.

PestPro does not provide advice regarding specific pesticide products, application rates, or treatment protocols. Always consult a licensed pest control professional for recommendations specific to your property and pest issue.
